# SCvx MPC logo concepts

The PNGs record three concepts generated with the built-in image generator. They use opaque near-black backgrounds with white, silver and electric blue. The website uses `roadster/web/public/assets/scvx-mark.svg` with live text; the README uses the same mark in a dark wordmark lockup. The prompts below record the original requests.

## 1. Trajectory

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: original logo concept for the SCvx MPC Demos website, an interactive car stunt and rocket landing model predictive control simulator. Create one polished standalone logo, with a compact symbol on the left and the exact wordmark "SCvx MPC" with smaller "Demos" below on the right. Preserve capitalization exactly: S C lowercase v lowercase x, space M P C. Flat crisp vector-like graphic, clean mathematical geometry, confident sans-serif typography. Pure white primary shapes and wordmark, flat silver secondary lettering, one restrained electric-blue #4da3ff trajectory accent, designed for a near-black #05070a aerospace mission-control website. Entirely flat fills. SpaceX-inspired color palette only; keep an original SCvx identity, do not use the SpaceX logo or its lettering. Solid opaque near-black #05070a background. Clean perfectly filled white letterforms, sharp edges, smooth uninterrupted shapes; no distressing, no noise, no speckles, no scratches, no textures. Large clean margins. No mockup, no photography, no emoji, no glossy effects, no gradients, no extra slogans, no border. Concept 1: an elegant looping predicted trajectory forms an abstract letter S inside an open circular arc, with three tiny control knots and a single precise arrowhead. Suggest iterative optimization and controlled flight. Bold simple memorable silhouette, readable as a small site icon. Balanced horizontal lockup.
